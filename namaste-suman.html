HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Namaste Suman Communication located?

We are situated at Gaindakot-5, Nagarpalika Chowk, Nawalparasi East, Gandaki Province — right next to the Gaindakot Municipality office, easily reachable on foot or vehicle.

Can I generate my passport photos online before visiting?

Yes! Use our free online Passport-Size Photo Generator tool to preview and prepare your photo. Then visit our counter for instant printing on high-grade photo paper.

Can I send my documents via WhatsApp or Email to print?

Absolutely. You can send your documents or PDF files to WhatsApp (+977 9866078388) or email namastesuman.com@gmail.com, and we will print them ready for pickup.

What official online forms do you help process?

We handle citizenship recommendations, National ID (NID), e-Passport appointments, Lok Sewa Aayog, Nepal Police/Army forms, land registration documents, and utility payments.
